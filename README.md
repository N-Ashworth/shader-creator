# shader-creator
A Shadertoy-style shader editor

Shadertoy is cool, but it isn't the best for art (just animations).
- There's bad jpeg compression, especially on videos
- Only 4 buffers!?!
- Can't upload your own textures
- Videos capture low fps (in other words it HAS to be real time)
- Hard to share an animation outside of the official Shadertoy site
And some others.

So I wanted to make Shadertoy but for art (I haven't settled on a name yet).
To be clear, *THIS IS NOT REPLACING SHADERTOY*.
It won't have realtime rendering or anything, and it won't have a cloud where people can share shaders (The way you share will be on other art-sharing sites, as it will easily be able to capture as image and video).
